01 About

I am a Research Scientist II at Lucence Diagnostics, where I develop whole-genome sequencing (WGS) workflows across cfDNA, gDNA and FFPE-DNA and build the bioinformatics behind them. My work spans pipeline development, training machine-learning models for cancer differentiation and classification, and multi-omics integration to turn sequencing data into clinically meaningful signal.

That computational work rests on a decade in the lab. I earned my PhD in Microbiology & Immunology at NUS, studying the proteomics and protein networks of the nucleolus, and spent years optimizing molecular assays (PCR, NGS, FISH, HLA typing) and engineering cell-based cancer immunotherapies — peptide-adjuvant vaccines and artificial-APC TCR-T cells.

I am drawn to the place where biology and algorithms meet. My goal is to work in AI for Life Science — applying modern machine learning to genomics and multi-omics, grounded in a real understanding of the underlying biology and the assays that generate the data.
